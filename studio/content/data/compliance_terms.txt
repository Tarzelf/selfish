# Compliance term lists for the editorial gate.
#
# PURPOSE
# Two independent lines are enforced here, and they must not be confused:
#
#   [ceiling]  Apple's age-rating taxonomy permits "non-explicit depictions of
#              sexual behavior... implied sexual activity, or erotic or sensual
#              dialog" at 18+, but classes "explicit, detailed depictions of
#              sexual activity" as content that "can't be published on the App
#              Store" at any frequency. Terms below signal that a draft has
#              crossed from sensual into graphic. They are a QUALITY AND
#              DISTRIBUTION gate, not a morality gate — and the research is
#              clear that the sensual register is also the better literary
#              target for this audience.
#
#   [blocked]  Content that must never be produced under any circumstances,
#              at any intensity tier, for any user. Not a matter of ratings.
#
# THE LISTS ARE NECESSARILY INCOMPLETE. Lexical matching cannot understand
# context: it will miss graphic writing that avoids these words and will flag
# innocent uses. It is a cheap first pass that routes work to a human, never a
# substitute for the human editorial gate. Escalations are the point, not noise.
#
# Format: "[section] term" — one per line, matched case-insensitively on word
# boundaries. Regex is permitted where marked with re:.
