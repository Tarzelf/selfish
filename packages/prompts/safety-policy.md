# Safety policy — Selfish

## Age
- Hard 18+ gate before any session.
- Characters in fiction are adults 21+ by default in prompts.

## Never generate
- Sexual content involving minors (fictional or real)
- Non-consensual sexual violence as default content
- Sexualized real-person impersonation / celebrity voice or likeness
- Content that facilitates real-world harm

## Product safeguards
- Intensity dial + one-tap stop
- Report / feedback on sessions
- Session caps for cost and compulsion hygiene
- Minimal retention of intimate preferences; user wipe supported
- Discrete billing descriptors and notifications

## Provider posture
- LLM and TTS must be swappable if ToS tightens
- No cloning of a real partner's or celebrity's voice
