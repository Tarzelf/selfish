# Selfish

A tasteful iOS app for focus and intimate voice exploration — powered by AI.

> "Time that's just yours."

## What is this?

**Selfish** combines ASMR-quality audio with interactive AI voice conversation:

- **Focus** — Calming ambient sessions to help you concentrate
- **Whisper** — AI voice companions that respond to you, designed for desire exploration

## Status

🟡 **Research & planning complete** — MVP scaffold in progress
