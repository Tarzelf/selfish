# Review persona — Maya

Use Maya to accept or reject every screen, line, and pricing idea. If the work would make her delete the app in the first ninety seconds, it is not done.

## Who she is

Maya is 32. She lives in a one-bedroom she is proud of. She has a job that uses her voice all day and a group chat that would be too much if she told the whole truth. She reads smut on her phone under a dim brightness. She has opened Quinn once, Dipsea once, and a “AI girlfriend” ad she closed so fast she felt briefly ashamed of the thumbnail.

She is not prude. She is **wary**. Wary of being sold a male fantasy with better kerning. Wary of a chatbot that will ask her to type what she wants before she has been given anything. Wary of a charge that says something she would not want on a lock screen. Wary of being made grotesque.

She wants, privately, to be wanted with intelligence. She wants a voice that does not rush. She wants to put the day down and feel her body without a performance review.

## The first-listen review she would write

I almost didn’t download this. The category is a graveyard of apps that think “for women” means pink lighting and a man who says *good girl* like he learned it from a spreadsheet.

Selfish did not do that.

The age gate was quiet. The first question was “what would feel like a gift tonight,” not a kink census. I said I wasn’t sure. The voice — low, unhurried — said I didn’t have to know yet. I believed it.

I listened to *The Door Ajar* with the transcript off and the room dark. It was closer to a short story spoken against my ear than to porn. I was not asked to answer. I was not shown a body. When it got warmer I could have opened the door further. I didn’t, and the app did not pout.

The last three minutes were aftercare. Water. The lamp. No joke, no upsell, no “rate your orgasm.” I stayed until the end, which I never do.

If they ever turn this into a chat that makes me work, or a feed of other people’s moans, I will leave. Tonight it felt like an hour that was actually mine.

— Maya, 32

## Her vetoes

- Thumbnails of bodies, even tasteful ones
- “Daddy,” ageplay, school, anything that smells like a minor
- A chatbot as the home screen
- Heat she did not ask for
