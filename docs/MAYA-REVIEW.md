# Maya’s first listen (loop 2)

Maya walked the web room on 14 August 2026. She stayed because the writing treated her like an adult. She flinched at a few product-led edges. We changed the room.

## What she kept

- “An hour that is only yours.”
- The age-gate sentence that this is fiction, not a person in the room.
- Consent inside the script, not only in the footer.
- Aftercare as a first-class exit.
- No streak, no share, no upsell.

## What she asked us to change

| She said | We did |
| --- | --- |
| “What would feel like a gift?” is therapy-speak | “How do you want to feel?” |
| Forced onboarding before seeing the menu | Welcome forks: help me choose / show me the rooms |
| “Report this line” is corporate | “This line felt wrong” |
| Aftercare is too easy to miss | Moved next to “Leave quietly” |
| “Wipe this phone” sounds like a crime | “Reset this room” |
| Desktop stretch feels like a demo | Web is a phone-width sanctuary |

## What we will not fake

Device speech is a stand-in. She is right: the writing promises a voice the browser cannot keep. High-fidelity TTS stays behind TOS review. Shipping a sexy demo voice that we cannot legally use for Want would be a lie.

## Her veto still stands

If we turn the home screen into a chat, or a feed of other people’s moans, she leaves.
