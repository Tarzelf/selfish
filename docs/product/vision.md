# Product Vision — Selfish

## Promise
A tasteful, women-first listening experience: AI writes a scene shaped around her desire; a high-fidelity voice performs it privately in her ears.

## Who it is for
Adult women who want intimacy, fantasy, and calm arousal through audio — especially those underserved by visual porn and frustrated by one-size-fits-all libraries.

## Who it is not for
- Minors (hard block)
- People seeking non-consensual or illegal content
- (MVP) Male AI girlfriend chat replacement — later, if ever, as separate mode

## Core loop
Mood + intensity → personalized script → intimate TTS → controlled listening → soft aftercare → optional continue thread

## Principles
1. **Agency over spectacle** — she controls pace and stop.
2. **Taste is the product** — editorial rails beat raw model output.
3. **Privacy is sacred** — discrete, deletable, never ad-targeted on desire.
4. **Voice must feel close** — if TTS fails the intimacy test, we don't ship louder sleaze to compensate.
5. **Honest distribution** — spicy generative core ships where policy allows (web-first); we don't gut the product to appease a store.

## MVP (lovable, small)
- 18+ gate
- Mood + intensity + voice pick
- Generate 8–12 min session (LLM → TTS)
- Polished player (play/pause, scrub, intensity note, stop)
- Save last N sessions for replay
- Account + subscription stub (web)
- Safety filter + report

## Explicitly out of MVP
- Social/feed/creators
- Live conversational voice agent
- Couples accounts
- App Store release
- Unlimited generation
- Image/video

## Success metrics
- Session completion rate
- "Felt for me" rating
- D1 / D7 return
- Paid conversion after 2–3 strong sessions
- Support tickets about creepiness (should be ~0)

## Brand
Working title **Selfish**. Alternative shortlist if user tests fail: Private, Hush, Low Voice, Only You, After Hours.

Visual: night intimacy, expressive typography, atmospheric light — not porn-tube, not purple-AI SaaS.
