# Home, from what women actually say

The Close home was inventing atmosphere ("He's already here." / "other mouths" / HEADPHONES ON). That is a man walking into her house. It is not what women in this category ask for.

## Sources

- Quinn founder, on record: top stories are **aftercare, apology, praise, body worship** — emotional connection, not the act ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/smut-audio-erotica-growing-trend-quinn-celebrities-collaborations-rcna263065)). 75–80% of listeners are women.
- Dipsea: marketed as a **wellness choice she makes for herself**; partners in-story sound trustworthy; no male-gaze visuals ([NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/07/31/style/dipsea-audio-stories.html), [Vox](https://www.vox.com/the-goods/2018/12/6/18126382/dipsea-app-porn-erotica-stories-millennial-women)).
- Academic read of BFE audio: the fantasy is **eroticized caretaking of her** — "let me take care of you" — not an uninvited arrival ([Anym & Saunders, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1080/23268743.2023.2220721)).
- Dual control (Nagoski / Bancroft): most women need **brakes off** (privacy, no shame, no surprise) before the accelerator works. See `03-science-and-content.md`.
- Maya (our target-user debate): mood picker must include **wanted / missed / in charge**; voices include F and NB; "women-first with only letter circles is for some women."
- Listeners, in their words: "it's the trust, the banter, the tension" (Hel, NBC); "it's all in your head" (Dazed); "a judgement-free way to explore" (Mattie, Dazed).

## What the home does now

1. **She names the feeling first.** Wanted, adored, comforted, teased, missed, in charge — only moods we can actually play.
2. **The voice has a name.** Jasper, Noor, Ash — not "other mouths."
3. **Copy is her agency.** "You closed the door. He stayed." not "He is already in the room."
4. **Recycles stay, demoted.** Again / slower / closer / aftercare are the habit mechanic. They are not the headline.
5. **Privacy is stated, not shouted.** "Private on this device. Headphones if you have them." — not a porn-site command.

Until a real listening panel of 8–15 women scores first-open emotion, this is the research-backed default — not a vibe.
